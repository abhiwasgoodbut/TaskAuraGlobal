Privacy Policy & Data Governance

TaskAura Global Consultancy Pvt. Ltd. is committed to uncompromising standards of confidentiality, data integrity, and privacy for all our enterprise clients, business partners, and platform visitors worldwide.

Effective & Last Updated: September 2026

01 Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy sets out how TaskAura Global Consultancy Pvt. Ltd. (“TaskAura”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) collects, processes, protects, and stores personal and enterprise operational information.

Operating from our headquarters in Indore, Madhya Pradesh, India, along with satellite operational coordination across Singapore, Canada, Australia, the United Kingdom, and Denmark, we serve as an integrated operational extension for businesses globally across Technology, AI Automation, and Finance.

02 Information We Collect

We collect information only to the extent necessary to evaluate engagements, enter into contracts, execute backend workflows, and ensure the operational continuity of our services:

  • Direct Business Information: Your name, official title, company entity, business email address, contact telephone numbers, and corporate billing credentials when you engage our team or complete our inquiry forms.
  • Project & Workflow Information: Technical specifications, process maps, credentials, API endpoints, and accounting/bookkeeping data provided under bilateral Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) to facilitate service delivery.
  • Automated Telemetry & Device Data: Standard web log records, IP addresses, browser specifications, operating system versions, and session metrics gathered to maintain platform stability and protect against malicious cyber traffic.

03 How We Use Your Data

All data collected by TaskAura is handled under strictly defined, lawful business purposes:

  • Delivering tailored technology development, software engineering, and AI automation models.
  • Executing accounting, ledger reconciliation, financial reporting, and payroll management workflows.
  • Providing dedicated customer success support and proactive performance analytics.
  • Complying with statutory accounting, taxation, anti-fraud, and corporate governance obligations globally.

Our Commitment: TaskAura does not sell, monetize, rent, or trade client data, proprietary codebases, financial ledgers, or personal information to data brokers, ad networks, or any unauthorized third parties under any circumstances.

04 Confidentiality & Client Intellectual Property

We recognize that our role as an operational partner requires handling sensitive business assets. Every engagement is bound by comprehensive Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs). All client intellectual property, internal documentation, trade secrets, customer databases, and proprietary algorithms remain the exclusive property of the client at all times.

TaskAura enforces strict logical network isolation, air-gapped repositories where required, and isolated development environments to prevent any cross-contamination of client data.

05 Enterprise Security Standards

We deploy institutional-grade defensive technical and organizational safeguards:

  • Encryption Standards: TLS 1.3 encryption for all data in transit across public networks, and AES-256 bit encryption for stationary databases and sensitive backup snapshots.
  • Access Control: Granular Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), multi-factor authentication (MFA), and principle of least-privilege (PoLP) access enforcement.
  • Auditability: Comprehensive access logging, continuous vulnerability scanning, and periodic security code reviews.

06 Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our platform uses minimal, privacy-conscious cookies and web beacons:

  • Essential Cookies: Required to enable navigation, security authentication, and proper interface display.
  • Performance & Analytics: Aggregate statistics (such as Google Analytics / Meta Pixel) used solely to evaluate page performance, monitor broken links, and optimize website responsiveness.

You may adjust your browser preferences at any time to decline or purge non-essential cookies without affecting your access to general website content.

07 International Data Transfers

As TaskAura coordinates projects across international boundaries with satellite operations in Singapore, Canada, Australia, the UK, and Denmark, data may be accessed across jurisdictions.

Whenever cross-border transfers occur, we implement recognized contractual safeguards, Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), and data protection agreements to ensure your information receives protection equivalent to the strictest international benchmarks (including GDPR, UK GDPR, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act).

08 Your Data Rights

Depending on your governing jurisdiction, you are entitled to exercise the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access: Obtain confirmation of whether your data is being processed and request a copy.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate, obsolete, or incomplete records.
  • Right to Erasure: Request the deletion or permanent scrubbing of personal data when retention is no longer contractually or legally required.
  • Right to Restriction & Objection: Object to processing on legitimate grounds or restrict temporary processing.

To submit a data access or deletion request, email our compliance team at rohitsingh@taskauraglobal.com. Requests are reviewed and addressed within 30 days.

09 Policy Revisions & Notifications

We review and update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our operational procedures, international legal statutes, or new technology capabilities. Any modifications will be posted here with an updated effective date.
